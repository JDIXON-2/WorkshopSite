Associate Professor and Turing Fellow. She leads the Cooperative AI Lab at KCL, working on reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and scalable alignment. Her research on reward design, learning under uncertainty, and human–AI coordination directly addresses the workshop’s interest in preference feedback modelling and adaptive data collection. Recent work on LLM training via verifier-free RL and evaluating generalisation in LLM-based agents connects directly to the workshop’s focus on post-training under evolving feedback.
