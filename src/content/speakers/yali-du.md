Yali Du heads the Distributed AI Group and leads the Cooperative AI Lab at King's College London. A Turing Fellow and ELLIS member, her research focuses on cooperative and safe multi-agent learning, human–AI coordination, and value alignment. She received the AAAI New Faculty Highlights Award and was named a KAUST Rising Star in AI (2023). Dr Du serves as Senior Area Chair for AAMAS and Area Chair for NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and IJCAI.
