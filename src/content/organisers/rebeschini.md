His research focuses on the statistical and theoretical foundations of machine learning, with particular emphasis on reinforcement learning, optimization, and implicit regularization. He is the recipient of an ERC Consolidator Grant and an Amazon Research Award. Patrick has organised numerous scientific events, including the Mathematics of Machine Learning Summer School at the [Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/). He is a Founding Member and current Director of the [Oxford ELLIS Unit](https://www.ellis.ox.ac.uk/).
