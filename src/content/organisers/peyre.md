CNRS Senior Researcher at the Department of Mathematics and Applications of École Normale Supérieure. His research lies at the interface of applied mathematics, optimal transport, machine learning, and the life sciences, with contributions spanning optimization dynamics, ResNets, transformers, and single-cell genomics. Recipient of three ERC grants, the CNRS Silver Medal, the Blaise Pascal Prize of the French Academy of Sciences, and the Magenes Prize of the Italian Mathematical Union. He has co-organised five NeurIPS workshops on Optimal Transport and Machine Learning, and has served as area chair for ICML, NeurIPS, and ICLR.
