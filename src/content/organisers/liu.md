Tenure-track associate professor at the Institute of Natural Sciences and School of Mathematical Sciences, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and a Global Visiting Professor at the Technical University of Munich, Germany. He was previously an assistant professor at the University of Warwick, UK (FAM Division, affiliated with DIMAP), and before that a postdoc researcher at KU Leuven and EPFL from 2019 to 2023. He received his Bachelor's degree in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2014 and his PhD from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2019. His research studies the foundations of modern machine learning from the perspective of learning efficiency, nonlinearity, and post-training. He is a recipient of the AAAI New Faculty Award and Rising Stars in AI recognition. He was the main organizer of the [NeurIPS 2024 Fine-Tuning Workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/neurips2024-ftw/home).
