Faculty member affiliated with the University of Warwick and Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research studies the foundations of modern machine learning from the perspective of learning efficiency, nonlinearity, and post-training. Recipient of the AAAI New Faculty Award and Rising Stars in AI recognition. Main organiser of the [NeurIPS 2024 Fine-Tuning Workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/neurips2024-ftw/home).
