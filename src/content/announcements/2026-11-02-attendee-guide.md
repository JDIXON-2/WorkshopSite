## Registration

Registration is handled by NeurIPS, not the workshop organisers, and fees are paid by the authors.

### How to register

1. Go to the Paris section of the NeurIPS 2026 author registration page. Paris is handled separately from Sydney and Atlanta, so the general page only shows those sites and Virtual.
2. Sign in to [neurips.cc](https://neurips.cc) with the same email address that appears on your OpenReview submission. Your presenting-author status is linked to that account.
3. Complete registration and payment.

### Reserved places

NeurIPS gave the workshop a limited number of reserved registrations, fewer than the number of accepted papers. The deadline for reserved seats was 30 October 2026.

- If you received an invitation email from the Paris registration team, you had a reserved place.
- If you did not, you were not allocated one.
- The workshop's complimentary registrations have all been assigned.

If you still need a place, email the NeurIPS Workshop Chairs at [workshop-chairs@neurips.cc](mailto:workshop-chairs@neurips.cc) and copy [contact@fllmpt-work.shop](mailto:contact@fllmpt-work.shop). We will support your request, but the decision rests with NeurIPS.

### Already registered?

If you hold a NeurIPS registration that covers the workshops, you can attend and present as normal.

## Presenting

Orals can be given in person or virtually; posters must be presented in person.

| Format | Length | Virtual option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oral | 10-minute talk + 5 minutes of questions (subject to final timing) | Yes. Confirm in person or virtual with the organisers, or the slot may be reallocated |
| Poster | Poster session | No. We don't have the resources for virtual posters |

### If no author can attend

Your paper keeps its accepted status and stays listed on the workshop website and OpenReview. You don't need to update anything.

## Posters and visas

For poster requirements and visa letters, follow the official NeurIPS 2026 guidance at [neurips.cc](https://neurips.cc).

- **Poster size and orientation:** use the NeurIPS 2026 poster guidelines on the main conference site.
- **Visa invitation letters:** NeurIPS issues these once your registration is complete. See the Paris registration information on [neurips.cc](https://neurips.cc), or contact the Paris registration team.
